# Colour palette attribution

Two palettes from the cpt-city collection are used. The full cpt-city tree is
not redistributed here: it aggregates palettes under differing terms, and only
these two are needed.

| File | Palette | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ncar.cpt` | NCAR | cpt-city, `gist/ncar` | physics-residual bands, `fig_residualmap.sh` |
| `qual-mixed-12.cpt` | Statistik Stadt Zuerich, mixed 12 | cpt-city, `ssz/qual-mixed-12` | distribution inset, `fig_residualmap.sh` |

Bathymetry uses `oleron` from Crameri's Scientific Colour Maps, and the bending
moment uses `vik`; both ship with GMT 6 and are not duplicated here.

BEFORE PUBLISHING: confirm the redistribution terms of each palette above at
its cpt-city page. If either may not be redistributed, delete the file, note the
download location here instead, and change the script default to a GMT master
palette.
